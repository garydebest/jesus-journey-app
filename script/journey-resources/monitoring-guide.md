**Title:** Encouraging Broad Participation

A strong response rate helps leaders listen more carefully to the church as a whole. The Jesus Journey target is participation from at least 50% of the total adults age 16 and over that your church has entered in the dashboard.

**Check progress regularly**

- Check the dashboard two or three times each week.
- Review the response count against your minimum target.
- Note your planned close date.
- Consider whether people have had enough clear opportunities to participate.

**If participation is slow**

- Ask the lead pastor to make a personal invitation.
- Ask ministry and small-group leaders to remind people personally.
- Repeat the invitation through more than one channel.
- Make the survey code and link easy to find.
- Explain that individual responses are confidential.
- Offer the paper-survey option and help people who need technical support.
- Consider extending the collection period if the congregation needs more time.

**Avoid**

- Publicly shaming people who have not responded.
- Treating the survey as a test of loyalty.
- Pressuring people to answer in a particular way.
- Drawing conclusions before a sufficiently broad response has been received.
