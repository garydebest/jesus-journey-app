**Title:** Jesus Journey Church Survey Coordinator Guide

**Welcome**

Thank you for serving as your church’s Jesus Journey Survey Coordinator. Your role is not to carry the process alone. Your role is to help your church prepare well, communicate clearly, encourage broad participation, and guide leaders through each step of the survey journey.

Jesus Journey is a tool for listening. It helps your church see strengths to celebrate and questions to explore as people grow in trusting, experiencing, reflecting, and serving Jesus. The survey is not a scorecard for pastors, staff, ministries, or individuals. It is a starting point for prayerful conversation.

**Your responsibilities**

- Serve as the primary operational contact for the survey.
- Attend the Jesus Journey Survey Orientation.
- Keep the survey timeline and action plan current.
- Coordinate preparation with the lead pastor and key leaders.
- Organize launch and reminder communications.
- Make sure people who need help or paper access can participate.
- Monitor response progress.
- Help leaders prepare to receive the reports.
- Help schedule the results debrief.
- Keep key materials, reports, and next steps organized.

**What success looks like**

A successful survey process is not measured only by a high response count. It includes:

- Clear senior-leader support.
- A well-prepared coordinator and leadership team.
- Broad, respectful invitations to all adults age 16 and over.
- Careful communication about confidentiality.
- A response level of at least 50% of the adult total entered for the survey.
- A leadership team willing to listen to the results with humility.
- A plan to report back appropriately to the church.

**Your survey timeline**

1. Prepare leaders and communications.
2. Attend the orientation.
3. Confirm the timeline and adult total.
4. Soft launch with key leaders.
5. Launch with the whole church.
6. Monitor participation.
7. Send reminders.
8. Decide whether to extend or close.
9. Review reports.
10. Book and prepare for the results debrief.

**Privacy and trust**

Individual survey responses are confidential. Church leaders receive group-level results, not a list of individual answers. Explain this clearly and consistently. People participate more freely when they trust that their personal responses will not be used to identify, judge, or compare them.

**When you need help**

Contact Jesus Journey when:

- You are unsure how to set a realistic adult total.
- Participation is low and you need help deciding whether to extend.
- A participant cannot access the survey.
- You need help explaining the paper-survey option.
- You are unsure whether the survey is ready to close.
- You need help preparing leaders for the results.
