**Title:** Preparing for Your Jesus Journey Results Debrief

**Read with the right posture**

The survey report is a starting point for prayerful conversation, not a final verdict on your church. Read it with curiosity, humility, and care.

**Before drawing conclusions**

- Check the response count first.
- Look for patterns, not isolated numbers.
- Remember that every percentage represents people with real experiences.
- Celebrate strengths as well as opportunities.
- Treat written comments with care.
- Avoid broad conclusions from a very small group of respondents.
- Resist the urge to explain every result immediately.
- Bring questions, not just solutions, to the debrief.

**Questions to bring**

- What strengths are most important to celebrate?
- What patterns surprise us?
- What do we need to understand better before acting?
- What voices or experiences may need further listening?
- What one or two questions seem most important for our church’s next season?
