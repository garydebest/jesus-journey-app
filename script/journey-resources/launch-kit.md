#### Two weeks before launch: Sunday announcement

> In two weeks, our church will begin Jesus Journey, a survey that helps us listen carefully to our shared experience of spiritual growth. If you are age 16 or older, we will invite you to take part. The survey takes about 10–15 minutes, and individual responses are confidential. In the weeks ahead, we will explain how you can participate and why your voice matters.

#### One week before launch: Sunday announcement

> Next week, our church will begin the Jesus Journey survey. This is an opportunity for us to listen well, celebrate strengths, and better understand where we may need to grow together. We hope every adult age 16 and over will participate. Watch for the survey code and link next week.

#### Launch-day: Sunday announcement

> Beginning today, our church is taking part in Jesus Journey—a survey designed to help us listen carefully to our shared experience of spiritual growth. If you are age 16 or older, we invite you to participate. It takes about 10–15 minutes, and your individual responses are confidential. Our leaders will receive only group-level results. You can take the survey using the code shown on the screen or the link in this week’s email.
