**Title:** Preparing Your Leaders for Jesus Journey

**Why we are taking this survey**

Jesus Journey helps our church listen carefully to people’s experience of spiritual growth. It can help us recognize strengths, understand different needs, and identify questions worth exploring together.

This is not a test of whether our church is succeeding or failing. It is not a performance review of pastors, staff, ministries, or individuals. It is a tool for prayerful listening and wise conversation.

**What leaders need to do**

- Understand the purpose of the survey before promoting it.
- Participate personally.
- Speak positively and honestly about the process.
- Invite people age 16 and over to participate.
- Reassure people that individual responses are confidential.
- Encourage people who need paper or technical help.
- Avoid guessing at results before the reports are ready.
- Commit to listening carefully and responding thoughtfully afterward.

**Suggested leader script**

> Our church is using Jesus Journey to listen carefully to where people are experiencing growth and where they may need support. This is not about rating people or defending our current programs. It is an opportunity to learn together, celebrate what God is doing, and listen for wise next steps.
